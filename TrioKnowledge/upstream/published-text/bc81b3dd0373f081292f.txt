Trio is an open-source artificial pancreas system based on the OpenAPS (Oref0) algorithm. It uses your entered settings, carbohydrates, and historical data to automate insulin delivery and reduce the time spent managing your diabetes.  !!! important "Important"     Trio is a DIY, open-source community project. As such, the user must build and maintain their app with the support of the Trio community. Trio has not undergone evaluation by any regulatory agency and is for use at your own risk.
