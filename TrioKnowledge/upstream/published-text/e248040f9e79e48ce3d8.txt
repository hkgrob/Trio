Default: OFF When Skip Neutral Temps is enabled, Trio will not set neutral basal rates shortly before the hour, minimizing hourly pump alerts on Medtronic pumps. This can help light sleepers avoid alerts, but will delay basal adjustments. This will also only come into effect if SMBs are disabled.   For most users, leaving this OFF is recommended to ensure consistent basal delivery and loop calculation. If this option is enabled, loops will be skipped during the last 5 minutes of the hour.
