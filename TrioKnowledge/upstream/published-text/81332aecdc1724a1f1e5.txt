 Your profile ISF is not directly used to determine your ISF when Logarithmic Dynamic ISF is enabled. It influences the limits of what ISF is allowed. Adjusting your profile ISF when using Logarithmic Dynamic ISF will not have a direct impact on your sensitivity calculations. It will only have an impact on the Maximum and Minimum values allowed.  Profile ISF Tip  This is why the Desmos graphs are invaluable whenever adjustments are needed with Logarithmic Dynamic ISF.
