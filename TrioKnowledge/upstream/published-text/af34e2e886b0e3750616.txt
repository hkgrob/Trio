CR is essential for Trio because it determines:  Carbs on Board (COB) Corrections: How much insulin is needed for unabsorbed carbs still affecting your glucose Bolus Calculator Recommendations: The carb coverage component of total insulin needed Automatic Bolusing: When SMB (Super Micro Bolus) is enabled, CR affects insulin delivery for meals Carbs on Board (COB) Calculations: Combined with your current ISF, your CR is used to calculate how many carbs have been absorbed and no longer need to be accounted for in corrections  Direct Usage Unlike basal rates and ISF, which are modified by Autosens or Dynamic ISF, carb ratio is used directly by Trio's algorithm.
