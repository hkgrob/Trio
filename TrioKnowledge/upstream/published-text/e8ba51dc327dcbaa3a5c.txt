Warning Trio is a DIY, open-source community project. It has not been evaluated by any regulatory agency and is not approved by any healthcare authority. You build and run Trio at your own risk. To use Trio, you are required to build the application from the source code. This does not require substantial technical know-how but is a time-consuming process. You may need several sessions to complete your first build. Upon installation, you will follow an onboarding wizard in the app to configure your settings optimally. We strongly recommend using the New User Setup Guide as you walk through onboarding so you are able to easily reference any additional information you may need. If you find yourself stuck, the community is here to help on Discord or Facebook.
