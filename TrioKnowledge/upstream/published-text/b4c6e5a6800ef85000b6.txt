  On your Mac computer, open the app store.   Next, in the top left corner of the App Store, there is a search engine. Search for Xcode.    Select the Xcode application from the search results.   Review the available Xcode version.   If the Xcode version you require is higher than the Xcode version available in the App Store, you need to update your computer.    Select download. Be patient; depending on your download speeds, this could take a couple of hours.     The Xcode application will be available from your Launchpad or Applications file once the download is complete.
