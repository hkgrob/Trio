This button will show one of the following messages:  Continue Without Treatment: This means no COB or IOB will be added Log Carbs: Carbs will be logged, but no insulin will be delivered Log FPU: FPUs will be logged, but no insulin will be delivered Log Meal: Both carbs and FPUs will be logged, but no insulin will be delivered Log Meal/FPU/Carbs and Enact Bolus: Carbs and/or FPUs will be logged and insulin will be delivered Log External Insulin: Insulin will be added to IOB, but it will not be delivered
