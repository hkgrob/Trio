Carbohydrate ratio determines how much insulin you need to cover the carbs you eat. Getting your CR right is essential for:  Accurate meal bolus calculations Appropriate COB corrections Accurate COB determinations Effective SMB delivery for meals Overall glucose control after eating  Work with Your Healthcare Provider Always consult with your diabetes healthcare team when making significant changes to your CR or when you're unsure about adjustments. They can provide personalized guidance based on your complete medical history.
