When using Sigmoid, increasing your Adjustment Factor will cause your sensitivity ratio to increase and decrease at a faster pace as your glucose rises and falls. You can see in the graphs below how the curve gets steeper as the Adjustment Factor increases.   Sigmoid Adjustment Factor Tips  If you need Sigmoid to respond faster, increase the Adjustment Factor. If you need Sigmoid to respond slower, decrease the Adjustment Factor. This is a fine tuning tool, and should be adjusted only after your core settings have been established as accurate.
