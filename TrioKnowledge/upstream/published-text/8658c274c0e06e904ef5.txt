In the Dosing Calculation section, it starts with the Reduced Bolus and Super Bolus options at the top, if you have those enabled. Below that is the suggested bolus amount that Trio has calculated. Tap the "i" icon for more details and read the section below for more details on the information shared when you tap on this icon. If you check the "External Insulin" box, the bolus will be added to your IOB, but it will not be delivered by Trio.
