ISF is a foundational setting that determines how much insulin is needed to correct high glucose. Getting your ISF right is crucial for Trio to function effectively. While Trio adjusts your ISF dynamically through Autosens or Dynamic ISF, having accurate baseline ISF values ensures:  Safe and effective corrections Appropriate SMB delivery Smooth glucose control without rollercoasters Effective algorithm predictions and decisions  Take the time to test and refine your ISF carefully. It's one of the most important settings for successful automated insulin delivery.
