Default: 30 minutes Setting Limits: 30-180 minutes If UAM is enabled, this setting limits the size of each UAM SMB. One UAM SMB can only be as large as this many minutes of your current profile basal rate.   To calculate the maximum UAM allowed based on this setting, use the following formula:   \[ \frac{Max\ UAM\ Basal\ Minutes}{60} \times Current\ Basal\ Rate \] Warning Increasing this value above 90 minutes may impact Trio's ability to effectively zero temp and prevent lows. Tip If you are NOT entering carbs or bolusing for meals and using Trio as a Fully Closed Loop (FCL), your Max UAM Basal Minutes should be set higher than your Max SMB Basal Minutes
