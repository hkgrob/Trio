 A typed entry can result in mistyping a value and could result in unintended consequences within the algorithm. A picker ensures the setting is within the guardrails of the algorithm. Both Trio 0.2 and 0.5 (or higher) have guardrails, however when typed in 0.2, those values were ignored and the closest valid number was used.      !!! example         If you set your DIA to 2, Trio 0.2 would use a DIA of 5 without notifying you.
