Trio is not available to download from the App Store. Trio is an open-source, do-it-yourself automated insulin delivery application. Community members who wish to utilize Trio must build it themselves.  While building your application may initially seem daunting, information and support are abundant and available to help you progress down this pathway. Each step required to build Trio can be found within this documentation. Additional support can be found on the Trio Facebook group and Discord group.
