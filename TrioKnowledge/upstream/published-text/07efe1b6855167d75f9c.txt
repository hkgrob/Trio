This can, however, be achieved one of two ways:  Enable "Sensitivity Raises Target" which will automatically increase your target glucose when sensitivity ratio is below 100%. Enable "High Temp Target Raises Sensitivity" and save a custom temp target of <100 mg/dL (5.5 mmol/L) under adjustments that you can manually enable when recovering from a low glucose.
