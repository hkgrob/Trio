Bill has an ISF of 50 mg/dL/U. His current glucose is 250 mg/dL and his target is 100 mg/dL. How many units of insulin does Bill need to reach his target? Here is the formula: \[ \frac{Current\ Glucose - Target\ Glucose}{ISF} \] Calculate Bill's Insulin Dose: \[ \frac{250 - 100}{50} = \frac{150}{50} = 3\ units \] Answer Bill needs 3 units of insulin to reach his target glucose of 100 mg/dL.
