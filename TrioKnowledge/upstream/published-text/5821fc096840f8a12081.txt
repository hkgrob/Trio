Default: 10 units Setting Limits: 1-30 units  This is the maximum bolus allowed to be delivered at one time. This only limits manual boluses given on the Treatments screen. If you attempt to request a bolus larger than this, the bolus will not be accepted or proceed. So if your max bolus is set to 5U but you enter a 6U bolus, the "Enact Bolus" button will turn red and display a warning message. You will be unable to proceed until you lower your bolus to within this limit.  Tip Most set this to their largest meal bolus, then adjust if needed.
