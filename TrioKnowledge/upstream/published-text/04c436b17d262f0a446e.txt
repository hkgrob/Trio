Garmin watches only display data and do not allow interaction for adding carbs or insulin. Two components can be installed on Garmin watches: a watch face that continuously displays information, and a data field that shows glucose information during activities. Both components are independent of each other and need to be sideloaded onto the watch as described below.  No need for Garmin Connect in the background — the watch just needs to be connected to the phone that runs Trio. The watch face updates with Trio loop cycles.
