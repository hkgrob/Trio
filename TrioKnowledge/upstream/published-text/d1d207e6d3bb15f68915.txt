When you first use Xcode, you will need to add your Apple ID to the accounts within Xcode.    Open Xcode.   In the top left-hand corner of the screen, select 'Xcode'.   Next, select 'Settings'. This will open the Xcode 'Settings' window.   Next, ensure you are in the 'Accounts' tab.   Press the plus symbol in the bottom left-hand corner of the screen. Follow the prompts to add your Apple ID.
