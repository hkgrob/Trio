Default: OFF  Enabling this setting adds a "Super Bolus" Option to the bolus calculator. Once this feature is enabled, a Super Bolus Percentage setting will appear for you to set. This option adds this set percentage of your current basal rate to your suggested bolus amount in the bolus calculator. If you want to receive all of your current basal rate in addition to your suggested meal bolus, use 100% for your Super Bolus Percentage.
