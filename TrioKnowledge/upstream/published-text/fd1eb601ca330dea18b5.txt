Apple TestFlight Bug It doesn't happen to everyone but it has happened a few times over the years and only for people who have Automatic Update enabled for their TestFlight app. Symptom:  App has plenty of time before expiration A new build becomes available, for example from the automatic monthly rebuild User is told Trio Beta is not available  Solution:  Open TestFlight and turn off automatic installation for your app Install the previous version of the app on the phone manually (with monthly build, it should still be ok) Manually start a new build (if there were updates you want to get) Manually install the new update  More information:  Link 1: Apple forum Link 2: Stackoverflow thread
