 Choose a folder on your Mac and get the latest release from this GitHub repo. You need *.prg files for your watch model from the release and OpenMTP from OpenMTP to transfer them to the watch. Install and start OpenMTP. Connect your Garmin watch via a data USB cable to the MacBook and allow the USB connection. The Garmin watch USB mode should be set to MTP. Transfer *.prg files for your watch model to the /GARMIN/APPS folder. Wait for the transfer to finish and disconnect the watch. Add the Garmin watch in Trio settings: Settings -> Watch -> Garmin Watch -> Add Devices. Choose the Trio watch face on the watch and enjoy.
