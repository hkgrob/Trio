If you build frequently, you do not have to delete the profiles every time. When the build script asks if you want to "Ensure a Year?", you can skip that step. On the other hand, you may need to delete the provisioning profiles or saved Xcode information about a version of currently on your computer. The maintenance utilities found in the BuildSelectScript can be run to delete your provisioning profiles or clear derived data. Or you can use the individual commands in the next sections to do the same thing.
