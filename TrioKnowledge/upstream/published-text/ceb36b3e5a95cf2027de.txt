The Nightscout version must be 15.0.2 (or newer) to properly display the OpenAPS pill with Trio 0.5.x (or newer). Check your revision: Nightscout URL, Menu, scroll to bottom and examine the About section. If you transitioned from the Loop app, you must make some modifications to Nightscout before you will be successful viewing your Trio data in your Nightscout site. In Nightscout, you need to modify these config vars: Config Var Loop Trio ENABLE loop openaps SHOW_PLUGINS loop openaps SHOW_FORECAST loop openaps Remember to restart the Nightscout server (restart dynos) after updating these variables.
