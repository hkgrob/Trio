Build, Enable, Build Users' reports indicate that when building a new Apple Watch, you must first build the app with Xcode before the developer mode becomes available. So, plan to build with the watch paired, and then enable Developer Mode and build again. In addition to your phone, if you plan on using an Apple Watch, then you must activate 'Developer Mode' on the watch itself. To determine if 'Developer Mode' is activated, follow the following instructions:   Open the 'Settings' application on the watch.   In the 'Settings' application, scroll and choose 'Privacy & Security'.   Scroll to the bottom of the page, and under the 'Security' banner, you will see an option for 'Developer Mode';  If it says On - no further action is required. If it says Off, tap on the row and slide the slider to the green (enabled) position.    Your watch will then prompt you to restart the device.   After the restart, you will be prompted to turn on 'Developer Mode'.   You are now ready to begin building from Xcode onto this watch.
