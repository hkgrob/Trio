The bolus calculator in Trio starts with your basic dosage needed to address your current glucose reading. If your glucose is below your target, it will be a negative number. If your glucose is above your target, it will be a positive number. Let's walk through Bill's current bolus calculation, starting with the first step, Glucose Calculation.   Glucose Above Target: Bill's current glucose reading is 122 mg/dL. His target glucose is 83 mg/dL. Trio is currently using a calculated ISF of 59 mg/dL/U. How much insulin does Bill need to reach his target glucose? Here is the formula you will need: \[ \frac{(Current\ Glucose - Target\ Glucose)}{\mathit{IS}\mathit{F}} \] Calculate Bill's Glucose Calculation: \[ \frac{(122-83)}{59} = \] \[ \frac{39}{59} = \] \[ 0.66\ units \] Answer Bill needs 0.66 units to reach his glucose target without accounting for any other factors. Glucose Below Target: Bill's current glucose reading is 70 mg/dL. His target glucose is 83 mg/dL. Trio is currently using a calculated ISF of 90 mg/dL/U. How much insulin does Bill need to reach his target glucose? Here is the formula you will need: \[ \frac{(Current\ Glucose - Target\ Glucose)}{\mathit{IS}\mathit{F}} \] Calculate Bill's Glucose Calculation: \[ \frac{(70-83)}{90} = \] \[ \frac{-13}{90} = \] \[ -0.14\ units \] Answer Because Bill was below target, the bolus calculator estimates he has the equivalent of 0.14 units already present based on his current ISF. Therefore, Trio will subtract 0.14 units from his insulin needed in the bolus calculator. Trio takes many additional factors into account. Next, it will adjust for the amount of insulin already in his system.
