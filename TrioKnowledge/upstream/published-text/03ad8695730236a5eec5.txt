Default: 120 grams of carb Setting Limits: 0-300 g  This setting defines the maximum amount of Carbs On Board (COB) allowed at any given time for Trio to use in dosing calculations. If more carbs are entered than allowed by this limit, Trio will cap the current COB in calculations to this Max COB setting and remain at this max until all remaining carbs have shown to be absorbed or 6 hours has passed, whichever comes first. For example, if Max COB is 120g and you enter a meal containing 150g of carb, your COB will remain at 120g until the remaining 30g have replaced absorbed carbs.
