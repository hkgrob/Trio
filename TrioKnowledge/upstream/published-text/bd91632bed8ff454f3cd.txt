Default: 400% Setting Limits: 100%-500% This setting also restricts the maximum automated temporary basal rate Trio can set. At the default of 400%, it caps temporary basal rates to 4 times your current programmed basal rate. This limit works in conjunction with Max Daily Safety Multiplier. Trio will use the smaller of the two limits. It serves as a safety limit, ensuring no temporary basal rates exceed safe levels. Warning Increasing this setting is not advised
