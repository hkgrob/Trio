Your glucose trend (Delta) takes into account how much your glucose readings have changed over the last 15 minutes. If you are experiencing a steep rise or steep fall, this helps Trio counter that activity and prevent an over- or under-dose. Let's look at how Trio accounts for an increasing trend and a decreasing trend in glucose: Increasing Trend: Bill's last 3 glucose readings were steadily increasing for a combined change of +29 mg/dL. His current ISF is 59 mg/dL/U How will Trio treat this trend in glucose? Here is the formula you will need: \[ \frac{Delta}{\mathit{IS}\mathit{F}} \] Calculate Bill's Glucose Trend Calculation: \[ \frac{29}{59} = \] \[ 0.49\ units \] Answer Based on his current ISF, Bill needs 0.49 units to counter the current trend in his glucose readings. Decreasing Trend: Bill's last 3 glucose readings were steadily decreasing for a combined change of -15 mg/dL. His current ISF is 100 mg/dL/U How will Trio treat this trend in glucose? Here is the formula you will need: \[ \frac{Delta}{\mathit{IS}\mathit{F}} \] Calculate Bill's Glucose Trend Calculation: \[ \frac{-15}{100} = \] \[ -0.15\ units \] Answer Based on his current ISF, Trio will subtract 0.15 units to counter the current trend in his glucose readings. Next, Trio combines all of the dosage components that it has calculated into one full bolus recommendation.
