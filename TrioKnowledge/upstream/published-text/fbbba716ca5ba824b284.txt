Before commencing on Trio, it is crucial that users test and refine their basals, carb ratios, and insulin sensitivity factors.  Why? Trio is only as good as the information you give it. Basal, carb ratios, and insulin sensitivity factors form the basis for all computing by Trio's algorithms. If you have given Trio poor-quality information, you will see poor-quality output. This will likely present as unexpected behavior to the everyday user and result in a poor user experience.
