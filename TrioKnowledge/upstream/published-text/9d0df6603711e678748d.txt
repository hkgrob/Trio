When you use the "Browser Build" method, you must access a browser at least once every 90 days to update with the Browser. This is simple enough to do the steps on your phone in just a few minutes.  When you use Build with Mac: 1. When you first build. 2. Once per year, at the minimum, after that. However, if you decide to use a free Apple Developer account, you must rebuild every 7 days.  Important Periodically, developers will release updates to Trio. These updates may include new features or important safety updates. It is recommended that as updates are released, users update their Trio app.
