Default: OFF Insulin Peak Time reflects when insulin is most effective in lowering glucose, aka the peak of insulin activity. Toggling this setting on exposes the Custom Peak Time setting and allows you to customize that peak. Typically, this setting does not need to be adjusted. The one common case for adjusting insulin peak time is for Lyumjev users, where the programmed ultra-rapid insulin peak matches Fiasp's peak time rather than Lyumjev's.
