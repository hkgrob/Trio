Because insulin needs vary throughout the day due to circadian rhythms, hormone fluctuations, and activity patterns, Trio supports time-based basal schedules:  You can define different basal rates for different times of day Time slots are in 30-minute increments Maximum of 48 entries (covering 24 hours) The first entry must start at 00:00 (midnight) Each rate applies from its start time until the next scheduled rate  Example Basal Schedule: Time Basal Rate(U/hr) Why It Might Vary 00:00 0.6 Lower overnight needs 03:00 0.9 Dawn phenomenon (early morning glucose rise) 09:00 0.7 Daytime baseline 17:00 0.8 Evening increase
