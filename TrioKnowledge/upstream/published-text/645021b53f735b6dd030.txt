Known Limitations  The Tandem Mobi pump will either vibrate or beep every 5 minutes when using Trio, Loop, or AndroidAPS. This is a hard technical limitation due to the current behavior of the pump which open-source developers cannot bypass at this time. The pump either makes an audible beep or a vibration (which you choose is user-configurable) in response to every Temp Basal, Bolus, or other insulin-delivery command. During testing, some users found the vibration motor to be more audible than the beep, which can be partially muffled through clothing and device placement. We are hopeful that a future pump software release will give users the ability to turn this off.  Tandem Mobi support is now in an open beta for experienced Trioneers. During the open beta, the driver is still being tested and is not yet recommended for everyone.
