Basal rates are your baseline insulin delivery schedule that Trio uses as a foundation for its automated insulin delivery. While Trio adjusts these rates dynamically every loop cycle, having accurate scheduled basal rates ensures:  Correct IOB calculations Effective Autosens adjustments Appropriate algorithm behavior Safe insulin delivery within your physiological needs Accurate safety net is in place when Trio cannot communicate with pump  Take the time to test and refine your basal rates. They're one of the most important settings for successful automated insulin delivery.
