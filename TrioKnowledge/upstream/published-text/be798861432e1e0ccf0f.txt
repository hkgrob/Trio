Let's confirm that your computer is compatible with and meets the requirements for building Trio.  The iOS of the phone on which you wish to build Trio dictates the required macOS of your computer. At a minimum, the current release of Trio requires iOS 16.3 or higher, Xcode version 15 or higher, and macOS 13.5 or higher.  The table below lists the minimum requirements to build the current release of Trio. If your macOS or Xcode version is higher, you can build on a Mac.
