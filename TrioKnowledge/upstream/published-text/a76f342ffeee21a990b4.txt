  To activate 'Developer Mode', your phone must be turned on and plugged into the computer with the Xcode application open.   Your phone will then prompt you to trust the computer. Select 'Trust'.   Next, open your phone's 'Settings' application.   In the 'Settings' application, scroll and choose 'Privacy & Security'.   Scroll to the bottom of the page, and under the 'Security' banner, you will see an option for 'Developer Mode';  If it says On - no further action is required. If it says Off, tap on the row and slide the slider to the green (enabled) position.    Your phone will then prompt you to restart the device.   After the restart, you will be prompted to turn on 'Developer Mode'.   You are now ready to begin building from Xcode onto this phone.
