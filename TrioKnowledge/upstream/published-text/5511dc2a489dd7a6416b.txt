"Open Loop" means Trio cannot make any adjustments automatically. In open loop, the system relies solely on you to make any adjustments.   You can control your pump and bolus with the Trio app, but nothing can be done without your approval. This is often referred to as running in open loop. You will get no Trio-initiated protection from lows or highs while in open loop. Trio essentially becomes a fancy non-AID PDM with a bolus calculator.
