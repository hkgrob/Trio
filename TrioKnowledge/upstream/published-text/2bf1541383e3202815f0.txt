Carbohydrate Ratio (CR), also written as Insulin-to-Carb Ratio (I:C or ICR), is the amount of carbohydrates (in grams) that one unit of rapid-acting insulin can "cover" or neutralize. It's expressed as: grams per unit (g/U) For example, a CR of 10 g/U means that 1 unit of insulin will cover 10 grams of carbohydrates.
