Changing your Target Glucose either with an Override, Temp Target, or profile Target change, shifts the Sigmoid graph so that your Profile ISF is used at the new Glucose Target set.   Target Glucose Tip  Be aware that if you make a drastic change your Profile Target Glucose while using Sigmoid, you may find that your ISF suddenly seems too weak or too strong. Watch for a few days to ensure you don't need to increase or decrease your ISF to match the new Glucose Target.
