Default: 300% Setting Limits: 100%-500% This setting restricts the maximum automated temporary basal rate Trio can set. At the default of 300%, it caps temporary basal rates at 3 times your highest programmed basal rate of all your basal rates. This limit works in conjunction with Current Basal Safety Multiplier. Trio will use the smaller of the two limits. It serves as a safety limit, ensuring no temporary basal rates exceed safe levels. Warning Increasing this setting is not advised
