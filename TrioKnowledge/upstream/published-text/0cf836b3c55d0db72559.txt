Basal rates are the foundational insulin delivery settings in Trio. They represent the amount of rapid-acting insulin delivered continuously throughout the day to manage your glucose levels when you're not eating. Think of basal insulin as the background insulin your body needs to maintain steady glucose levels during fasting periods. Basal rates are measured in units per hour (U/hr). For example, a basal rate of 0.8 U/hr means you receive 0.8 units of insulin spread out over the course of that hour.
