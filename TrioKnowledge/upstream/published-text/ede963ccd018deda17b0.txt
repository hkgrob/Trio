Trio's bolus calculator uses your CR as part of the total insulin dose calculation: \[ Total\ Insulin = \frac{Current\ BG - Target}{ISF} + \frac{Carbs}{CR} + Trend - IOB \] The carb coverage portion specifically uses: \[ Carb\ Insulin = \frac{Carbs\ Entered + Current\ COB}{Carb\ Ratio} \] Where:  Carbs Entered: New carbs you're adding in the treatment screen Current COB: Carbs on board from previous meals still being absorbed Max COB Limit: Default 120g; prevents excessive insulin when carbs are not yet digested.
