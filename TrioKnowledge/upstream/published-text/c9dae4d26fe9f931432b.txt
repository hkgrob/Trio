Make sure your phone's battery is functioning optimally. Your phone will become a critical health device, and you want it to keep working.  Make sure a charger and cord are in your diabetes supplies Consider buying a battery pack, keep it charged, and add it to your travel bag  Low Power Mode Some people have reported that Trio continues working in the background (phone locked) even in Low Power Mode. Others have reported that they still get red loops. You can experiment to determine if your phone/iOS/app is able to maintain green loops in low-power mode. Otherwise, the best practice is to avoid Low-Power Mode.
