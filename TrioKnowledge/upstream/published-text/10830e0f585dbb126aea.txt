Yes. Trio does not require internet or cell coverage to work. So long as the Trio user has Bluetooth enabled on the iPhone, the CGM and insulin pump will continue working in conjunction with Trio.  Warning If you have chosen a CGM source that requires the internet (i.e., Nightscout or Dexcom Share), users must maintain an internet connection. If you enter an area without internet, you will lose your CGM readings and Trio will stop looping.
