AndroidAPS and Trio are based on the same algorithm (Oref) and base system (Artificial Pancreas System, APS). While there are several differences between AndroidAPS and Trio, the main algorithm is the same,  meaning you can expect Trio to behave the same way AndroidAPS does generally. Benefits of AndroidAPS  Custom automation Detailed reporting Working remote bolus (for caregivers, not working at the moment in Trio) A built-in learning program  Benefits of Trio  Trio works on your iPhone and Apple Watch Trio has dynamic CR support  Should I switch from AndroidAPS to Trio?  If you have been successful with AndroidAPS but prefer the Apple ecosystem, you can easily transition to Trio. If you've struggled to get stable BG with AndroidAPS, you may have the same issues with Trio.  Take some time to read the docs and fine-tune your settings. Once your settings are in order, consider switching to Trio.
