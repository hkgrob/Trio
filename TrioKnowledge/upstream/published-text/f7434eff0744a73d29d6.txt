Your actual carb coverage needs can vary based on:  Fat content: High-fat meals may need reduced upfront insulin (Reduced Bolus Option) Protein content: Large amounts of protein may require additional coverage Glycemic index: Fast-absorbing carbs vs. slow-absorbing carbs Fast-absorbing: Utilize the Super Bolus Option Slow-Absorbing: Utilize the Reduced Bolus Option    These factors affect meal timing and absorption, not necessarily your CR itself.
