If you notice an arrow pointing up and to the right beside the link:  This means a new tab or window (depending on your browser configuration) is opened when you click on the link Your original place in the docs is in the original tab, so you can return to your same position in the docs  If there is no upright arrow by a link, then the location is on the same page:  Once you have read that section, hit the back arrow in your browser to return to your original location
