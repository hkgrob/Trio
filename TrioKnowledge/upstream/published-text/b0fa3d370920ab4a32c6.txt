Statistics are calculated from data stored in Trio's database:  Glucose: Requires CGM data Insulin: Requires pump event history Looping: Requires loop execution records Meals: Requires logged carb entries  If you've recently started using Trio or reset your data, some statistics may not be available until sufficient data accumulates. Minimum data for meaningful statistics:  1 day: Today and Day views 7 days: Week view 30 days: Month view 90 days: 3 Month view
