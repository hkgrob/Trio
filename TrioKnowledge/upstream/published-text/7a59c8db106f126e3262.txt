To test if your ISF is accurate:  Choose a testing time: When you're "stuck" above target (around 150 mg/dL or 8.3 mmol/L) or by bringing yourself to a higher glucose with glucose tabs Prepare Trio for testing: Set Max IOB to 0 (Optional: Set Autosens Max and Min to 100% to avoid Autosens adjustments) Give a correction bolus based on your current ISF To determine your testing dose, follow the formula \(\frac{Current\ Glucose - Target\ Glucose}{ISF}\)   Wait 4 hours without eating or exercising Evaluate the result: Still above target after 4 hours: ISF is too high (too conservative) → DECREASE the ISF value Below target after 4 hours: ISF is too low (too aggressive) → INCREASE the ISF value At target after 4 hours: ISF is accurate   Return Max IOB to your previous setting after you've completed the test  Understanding ISF Adjustments  Lower ISF number = More insulin per unit of glucose correction = More aggressive Higher ISF number = Less insulin per unit of glucose correction = Less conservative  This can be counterintuitive at first!
