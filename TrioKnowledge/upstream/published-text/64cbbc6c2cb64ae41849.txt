Temporary Targets, also called Temp Targets, are glucose targets set by the user for a short period of time. Temp targets can be used to increase or decrease insulin delivery. There are multiple cases when a temp target may be useful. Below you will find a few examples. This list is not all-inclusive and you may find additional uses for temp targets as you use Trio. In addition to temp targets, Trio also has a more customizable feature called Overrides.
