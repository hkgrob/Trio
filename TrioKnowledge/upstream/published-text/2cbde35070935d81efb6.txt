Default: 30 min Setting Limits: 10-60 min  This determines how many minutes will be between individual Fat-Protein Unit Carb Equivalent (FPU) entries from a single Fat and/or Protein bolus calculator entry. The shorter the interval, the smoother the correlating dosing result. Increasing this setting may result in fewer FPU entries with larger carb values. Decreasing this setting may result in more FPU entries with smaller carb values.
